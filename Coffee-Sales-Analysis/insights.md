# 📊 Business Insights – Coffee Sales Analysis

## Executive Summary

This analysis explores coffee shop transaction data to understand revenue performance, customer behavior, and product demand patterns using Microsoft Excel.

---

## Key Findings

### ☕ Product Performance

* Coffee products contribute the largest share of total revenue.
* A small group of products generates the majority of sales, indicating a Pareto distribution.

### 🏪 Store Performance

* Sales distribution across locations remains balanced.
* Hell’s Kitchen and Lower Manhattan stores show slightly higher contribution.

### ⏰ Customer Behavior

* Peak sales occur during morning hours (8 AM – 10 AM).
* Customer demand decreases steadily after evening hours.

### 📅 Sales Trend

* Monthly revenue shows gradual growth, suggesting increasing customer engagement.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Increase staffing during morning peak hours.
* Promote lower-performing product categories to diversify revenue.
* Bundle popular coffee products for upselling opportunities.
* Optimize inventory planning based on hourly demand trends.
