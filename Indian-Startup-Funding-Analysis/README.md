# Indian Startup Funding Analysis (2015–2020)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Indian startup funding data from 2015 to 2020 using Microsoft Excel to uncover investment trends, industry dominance, investor behavior, and city-wise funding concentration.

The objective was to transform raw funding data into meaningful business insights through structured data cleaning, pivot analysis, lookup enrichment, and interactive dashboard development.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze funding growth trends over time
* Identify top industries attracting investment
* Determine leading investors by total funding
* Compare Angel vs Venture Capital participation
* Evaluate geographic concentration of startup funding
* Segment funding rounds by investment size

---

## 📈 Key Findings & Insights

### 📅 Funding Trend

Funding peaked in 2017 and 2019 before sharply declining in 2020, reflecting the impact of COVID-19 on investment activity.

### 🏢 Industry Dominance

E-commerce and Consumer Internet sectors attracted the largest share of capital, indicating strong investor confidence in digital-first business models.

### 🏙 Geographic Concentration

Bengaluru emerged as the top funding city, reinforcing its position as India's startup capital.

### 💰 Investor Influence

Westbridge Capital and SoftBank were among the highest funding contributors, showing strong institutional presence.

### 📊 Funding Structure

Venture Capital funding significantly outweighed Angel investment, highlighting a focus on scaling-stage startups.

### 💵 Deal Size Distribution

Most funding rounds fall in the 1–10M range, suggesting strong mid-stage investment activity.
